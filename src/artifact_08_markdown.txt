### L1 Operational Runbook: Incident Response & Emergency Escalation

1. **RPC Desynchronization**:
   - The daemon monitors slot progression. If the difference between local processed slot and network slot exceeds 150, an automatic WebSocket re-handshake triggers.
2. **Key Rotation**:
   - Funder, Recipient, and Arbiter can be rotated per escrow instance without protocol redeployment.
3. **Healthcheck Endpoint**:
   - `GET http://localhost:8080/health` returns `{"status":"UP","active_subscription":true,"network":"devnet"}`.
